# On Air Pets Character Prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The teal dragon and Maltese from Companion Animal Series v2 were retained. The remaining eight characters were generated as new transparent PNG assets.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: reusable transparent companion character sprite for a macOS camera and microphone detector
Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 define only the shared material, lighting, facial simplicity, and contemporary calm visual family. Preserve their soft minimal 3D language without copying either body shape.
Style/medium: soft minimal 3D illustration, clean rounded geometry, nearly no outline, matte velvety material, restrained diffuse shading, tiny facial marks, calm contemporary character design.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 transparent canvas, exactly one animal centered with generous breathing room, whole body visible, three-quarter view facing right.
Pose/expression: calm resting pose, both eyes closed as two tiny curved lines, peaceful tiny mouth.
Lighting/mood: soft warm studio light with a faint neutral contact shadow only.
Color palette: muted, gentle, low saturation.
Constraints: species recognition must come from a distinct outer silhouette; no interface cues, no accessories, no text, no frame, no colored square, no glow, no recording dot, no sound waves, no camera symbol, no watermark.
Avoid: copying the reference body shape, forcing a bean body, photoreal fur, busy texture, glossy plastic, oversized chibi head, humanlike pose, background scene, multiple animals, collage.
```

## Subject prompts

1. Siamese cat: a graceful muted blue-gray Siamese cat with a slim upright seated chest, tall triangular ears, a small wedge-shaped face, darker ears and muzzle, long front paws, and one elegant tail curling around the feet. Use a distinctly vertical feline silhouette with a narrow neck and tall ears. Do not use a low oval loaf body.

2. Fox: a muted rust-orange arctic fox with a compact crouched body, tall pointed ears, cream muzzle and chest, and an oversized plume tail wrapping in a broad crescent around one side. A triangular head plus a huge sweeping tail must define the silhouette. Keep the legs small but visible.

3. Lop rabbit: a warm oatmeal lop-eared rabbit with a small upright pear-shaped torso, very long soft ears hanging down on both sides, visible round haunches, tiny front paws, and a small cotton tail. Tall drooping ears and round seated haunches must create a rabbit silhouette distinct from the dogs.

4. Duck: a muted butter-yellow duckling with a small pear-shaped standing body, flat warm-orange bill, tiny tucked wings, and two visible orange webbed feet. Use a compact standing bird silhouette with a clear bill, short neck, and visible feet. Do not make it a flat oval.

5. Axolotl: a pale blush-pink axolotl with a low gently curved aquatic body, short rounded legs, a broad friendly head, three soft feathery external gill branches on each side, and a tapering translucent tail fin. The wide head, paired feathery gills, and long tail fin must make a clearly amphibian silhouette. Keep it low and horizontal without looking like a lizard.

6. Penguin: a small emperor penguin with an upright teardrop body, muted charcoal back, warm ivory belly, tiny side flippers, short dark beak, and two small webbed feet. Use a clean vertical teardrop bird silhouette with flippers held slightly away from the body. Use no round mammal ears or tail.

7. Turtle: a muted sage and warm clay turtle with a broad low domed shell, a small head extending forward on a short neck, four visible paddle-like feet, and a tiny tail. The shell must dominate as a wide architectural dome with the head clearly separate. Do not use a generic oval animal body.

8. Seal: a soft cool-gray harbor seal with a long tapered reclining body, round forehead, short muzzle, two small front flippers, and paired rear flippers gently splayed behind. Use a smooth horizontal comma silhouette with clear flippers and no visible legs. The head should rise slightly above the long body.
